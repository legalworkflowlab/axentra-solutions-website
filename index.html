Delivery Model

How delivery is structured

The delivery model is configured around the work, systems, access requirements, review points and level of operating responsibility agreed with the client.

Define

Confirm the workflow, activities, responsibilities, systems, volumes, dependencies and intended outputs.

Configure

Agree the team structure, instructions, access, controls, review requirements, reporting and escalation routes.

Transition

Complete knowledge transfer, training, system readiness, pilot checks and initial delivery validation.

Run and Review

Manage the agreed workflow, maintain visibility over delivery and exceptions, and review the model using client feedback and operating information.

AI Controls

AI used within defined operating controls

Axentra can incorporate AI and automation into research, document preparation, classification, data handling, testing, reporting and other agreed workflow activities.

The approach depends on the client's tools, information requirements, security environment and review model. AI-assisted outputs remain subject to the validation and approval arrangements agreed for the engagement.

Client-approved environment

Use the tools, systems and access model approved for the engagement.

Defined use cases

Apply AI to agreed activities rather than introducing it across the workflow by default.

Human validation

Review outputs according to the risk, complexity and client requirements of the work.

Engagement Options

Engagements can begin at different points

Defined project

A specific backlog, transaction, review, remediation or implementation requirement.

Bounded pilot

A limited workflow used to confirm the scope, controls, access and delivery approach.

Managed workflow

Ongoing management of an agreed process through a defined team and operating model.

Dedicated delivery team

Structured capacity aligned to the required skills, systems, volumes and reporting model.
