Quality assurance

Quality is part of the delivery

Quality is not treated only as a final check.

Each engagement defines how work is prepared, reviewed, approved, escalated and reported. The review structure reflects the nature of the work, the level of risk, client requirements and the experience of the assigned team.

Clear quality criteria

Instructions, templates, checklists, expected outputs and escalation points are agreed and calibrated at the beginning of delivery.

Review layers

Production checks, supervisory review, sampling or independent QA are arranged according to the engagement and client requirements.

Exceptions recorded and resolved

Missing information, inconsistencies, unusual cases and unclear instructions are recorded, routed and tracked rather than resolved informally.

Findings used to improve delivery

Recurring findings help identify issues in intake, instructions, templates, training, systems, allocation, review or escalation.

Engagement-specific QA

Quality arrangements can include

The review and QA structure is agreed according to the project or managed workflow.

  • Initial sample validation
  • Calibration exercises
  • First-line checks
  • Supervisory review
  • Independent QA
  • Risk-based sampling
  • Exception registers
  • Rework tracking
  • Root-cause analysis
  • Corrective action
  • Trend reporting
  • Client feedback
  • Refresher training
  • Updates to instructions and checklists

Technology and accountability

Comfortable with technology. Accountable for the output.

Axentra works within client and approved platforms and uses technology, automation and AI where they improve delivery, consistency or visibility.

Technology does not remove accountability for the output.

Trained professionals review the work against source documents, client instructions, templates, playbooks and agreed quality criteria. Human judgement remains central where context, commercial meaning, legal language, exceptions or unclear information require review.

Technology can assist with

  • Document classification
  • Data extraction
  • Clause comparison
  • Draft preparation
  • Workflow routing
  • Tracking
  • Validation
  • Reporting
  • Identifying exceptions
  • Reviewing larger document sets

Client information is not entered into unauthorised public AI tools or used for independent model training.

Delivery insight

More than task completion

During delivery, Axentra records recurring errors, avoidable rework, missing information, unclear instructions, process delays and other patterns affecting quality or turnaround.

Where a pattern is relevant to the agreed scope, Axentra shares findings and improvement options with the client.

The purpose is to make useful delivery findings visible and help the client decide where improvement is worthwhile.

Recommendations can relate to

  • Intake forms
  • Data requirements
  • Templates
  • Playbooks
  • SOPs
  • Review checklists
  • Training
  • Work allocation
  • Handoffs
  • Escalation routes
  • Reporting
  • Quality sampling
  • Product logic
  • AI prompts
  • Extraction instructions
  • Test cases
  • User guidance

Changes to the client's process, product, instructions or systems are introduced only after client review and agreement.
