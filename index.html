How clients engage us

Support structured around the requirement

The delivery structure depends on the work, volumes, duration, required experience, systems, review requirements and client working hours.

On-demand support

Additional capacity for backlog, workload peaks, urgent deadlines, team leave or temporary resource gaps.

Defined projects

A team and delivery plan arranged around a specific scope, output, timeline and completion criteria.

Managed workflows

Ongoing support for recurring work with agreed instructions, service levels, quality checks, reporting and escalation routes.

Dedicated teams

Teams structured according to the required roles, experience, volumes, quality layers, supervision and time-zone coverage.

Secondments

Professionals assigned for an agreed period to work within the client's team, systems, instructions, reporting structure and business hours.

India and international delivery

Supporting clients across markets and time zones

Axentra delivers from India for clients in India and international markets.

Working hours and team coverage are agreed according to the client's location, business hours, deadlines and workflow requirements. This may include scheduled overlap with client teams, early or late working windows and planned coverage during client or local holidays.

Holiday and extended-hour coverage is arranged in advance through the engagement staffing plan.
