Legal Tech and AI Quality Support

Business-user testing and human review for legal technology

Axentra reviews whether legal, contract and property technology works as expected for the people using it and whether AI-generated information matches the source document.

The service covers business-user testing, AI clause and data validation, summary review, error trends and retesting. It is not a full software quality-assurance or engineering service.

Discuss Legal Tech Support

01

Business-user testing

Test the product through the user's work

Axentra tests whether the configured product works as expected for the legal, contract, property or business user.

This type of business-user testing is commonly referred to as user acceptance testing, or UAT.

It does not include source-code, penetration, performance or cybersecurity testing.

Business-user testing activities
  • Agreed user journeys
  • Document upload
  • Data fields
  • Workflow routing
  • Approvals
  • Alerts and reminders
  • User-facing reports
  • Issue records
  • Retesting after corrections

02

AI clause and data validation

Compare extracted information with the source

Axentra compares AI-extracted clauses and data with the source document.

Clause and data review
  • Correct clause or field
  • Correct value
  • Missing information
  • Incorrect classification
  • Conditions and exceptions
  • Amendments
  • Source reference
  • Commercial dependencies

03

AI summary validation

Check that the summary keeps the important detail

Axentra reviews whether a generated summary accurately reflects the source document and preserves relevant conditions, qualifications, dates, amounts and party responsibilities.

04

Error trends and retesting

Group repeated corrections and share what they show

Individual corrections are grouped to identify which fields, clauses, document types or wording patterns continue to produce errors or require high levels of manual review.

Axentra shares these findings with the client's legal, product or data team to support updates to extraction rules, prompts, playbooks and test examples. Updated outputs are then retested.

Axentra does not retrain AI models, develop AI, change source code or own release decisions.

Trend and retesting activities
  • Fields that repeatedly fail
  • Clauses that need frequent correction
  • Document types with recurring issues
  • Wording patterns linked to errors
  • Areas requiring high levels of manual review
  • Retesting after updates
