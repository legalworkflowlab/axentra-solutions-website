Case, Matter and Records Management

Records and matter workflows built for retrieval and review

Axentra can manage matter opening, document organisation, status logs, file indexes, reporting packs and closure records across defined legal and business workflows.

Discuss Records Management

Where this service may fit

Legal departments, operations teams and service portfolios where records are scattered, status is unclear or handovers depend on individual memory.

What Axentra can manage

  • Matter opening and file setup
  • Document indexing and naming routines
  • Status logs and ownership trackers
  • Missing-item follow-up
  • Handover notes
  • Closure and archive records

Potential outputs

  • Matter index files
  • Document maps
  • Status reports
  • Exception logs
  • Handover packs
  • Archive summaries

How an engagement can be structured

Work can begin with one matter type or repository, then extend into a records operating model with naming standards, ownership fields and reporting cadence.

Client inputs and decision points

Client teams provide matter rules, repository access, retention instructions and approval paths. Record retention decisions and regulated responsibilities remain with the client.
