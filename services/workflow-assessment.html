Workflow Assessment

Understand the work before proposing the team

Axentra reviews the work, volumes, information, systems, skills, working hours, client decisions and quality checks before proposing a delivery structure.

The assessment helps both teams agree what will move, what stays with the client and what is needed for a practical transition.

Assessment questions

Seven questions that clarify the work

Design-thinking methods are used where they help the teams understand users, handoffs and recurring problems. The approach depends on the project.

  1. 01What starts the work?
  2. 02What information and documents are needed?
  3. 03Which decisions stay with the client?
  4. 04Which activities move to the Axentra team?
  5. 05What systems and access are involved?
  6. 06What interrupts the ordinary process?
  7. 07What confirms that the work is complete?

A practical basis for scope, staffing and transition

  • Common issues
