Seven assessment questions

Define what moves, what stays and what proves completion

Design-thinking methods are used where they help clarify users, constraints, handoffs and operating evidence. The method is selected for the requirement rather than treated as a fixed ceremony.

  1. 01What event starts the work?
  2. 02What information and documents are required?
  3. 03Which decisions remain with the client?
  4. 04Which activities move into the delivery team?
  5. 05What systems and access are involved?
  6. 06What exceptions interrupt the ordinary process?
  7. 07What proves that the work is complete?
