Contract and Commercial Operations

Contract lifecycle and commercial process services

Services cover contract intake, drafting from approved templates, review coordination, execution, repositories, obligations, amendments and commercial data.

Commercial data includes fees, pricing, rebates, sponsorship payments, commissions, revenue shares, escalation mechanisms, service credits and other agreed financial terms, together with the conditions that determine when they apply.

Commercial data

A value is useful only when its conditions are clear

Axentra records the financial term together with the event, period, calculation, dependency, notice and owner that determine how it applies.

Financial terms

  • Fees and pricing
  • Fixed and variable charges
  • Revenue shares and percentage rent
  • Minimum guarantees
  • Escalation and indexation
  • Sponsorship payments
  • Rebates and credits
  • Incentives and commissions
  • Discounts and service credits
  • Payment milestones
  • Caps, floors and thresholds
  • Renewal and termination pricing
  • Taxes and withholding
  • Audit and reconciliation rights
