Commercial abstraction and logic

A commercial value without its governing logic may be misleading.

Operational data connects the value to the event, period, condition, exception, owner and evidence that determine how it applies.

  • Fees, fixed charges and variable charges
  • Usage charges and revenue share
  • Percentage rent and minimum guarantees
  • Escalations and indexation
  • Sponsorship payments
  • Rebates, credits and incentives
  • Commissions and discounts
  • Pass-through charges, service charges and operating expenses
  • Payment milestones, retention and holdbacks
  • Bonus payments, service credits and penalties
  • Liquidated amounts
  • Caps, floors and thresholds
  • Renewal pricing and termination payments
  • Taxes and withholding
