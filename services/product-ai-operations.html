Primary service line

Product and AI Operations

Domain-led testing, output assurance and improvement analysis for legal, contract and property technology.

A product may function technically and still fail where legal language, business rules and user behaviour meet. A clause may be extracted but classified incorrectly. A summary may omit a qualification. A commercial field may appear without the condition that determines when it applies.

Axentra supports product teams through domain-led testing, output validation, exception analysis and structured improvement feedback.

01

UAT and release assurance

Test the product through the work it is expected to support

Axentra provides domain and business UAT. This service does not constitute source-code, penetration, performance, security or automated testing.

Domain and business UAT scope
  • Requirement review and acceptance criteria
  • User journeys and workflow scenarios
  • Test cases, scripts and data
  • Document packs
  • Role and permission testing
  • Intake and workflow routing
  • Approval, reminder and escalation testing
  • Document linking and version testing
  • Search and filter validation
  • Reporting validation
  • Defect recording and reproduction steps
  • Screenshots and source evidence
  • Defect classification and prioritisation support
  • Retesting and regression testing
  • Release-readiness reporting

02

AI output assurance

Validate both the extracted field and its source context

AI clause extraction validation
  • Clause presence and absence
  • Classification and text boundaries
  • Parties, dates and defined terms
  • Cross-references and amendments
  • Order of precedence
  • Conditions, exceptions and qualifications
  • Rights, obligations and restrictions
  • Renewal, termination and notice
  • Commercial terms
  • Source-document linkage
  • Confidence flags
  • Missing, incomplete or incorrect extraction
AI-generated summary validation
  • Accuracy against source
  • Missing conditions and qualifications
  • Unsupported conclusions
  • Incorrect party attribution
  • Loss of defined-term meaning
  • Incorrect amendment treatment
  • Omitted dates, values or dependencies
  • Overstated rights or obligations
  • Simplification-induced ambiguity
  • Suitability for the intended user

03

Recurring-error analysis

Convert repeated corrections into structured feedback

Axentra does not treat every correction as an isolated review event. Findings are classified and translated into recommendations for product logic, prompts, extraction instructions, playbooks, test cases and review rules.

Patterns examined
  • False positives and false negatives
  • Missed clause structures and wording variants
  • Formatting issues
  • Amendment and document-hierarchy failures
  • High-review fields
  • Summary inconsistencies
  • Prompt and playbook gaps
  • Extraction-rule gaps
  • Confidence-threshold issues
  • Edge cases
  • Regression scenarios
Typical outputs
  • Error taxonomy
  • Field-level and clause-level findings
  • Trend analysis
  • Recurring exception report
  • Source examples
  • Logic and prompt recommendations
  • Playbook recommendations
  • Updated validation criteria
  • Edge-case library
  • Regression test pack
  • Before-and-after comparison
  • Release-readiness report

Recommendations are provided to the client's legal, product, operational, data or engineering team for review and implementation. Axentra does not claim to train or retrain AI models.
