Alternative legal and business process services

Support for legal, lending, servicing, commercial and technology teams

Axentra supports law firms, in-house legal and business teams, lenders, servicers, legal-technology companies, contract-technology providers and property-technology businesses.

Services are available through on-demand support, defined projects, managed recurring workflows, dedicated teams and secondments.
