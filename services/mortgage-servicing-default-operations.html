Mortgage Servicing and Default Operations

Support across loan records, default activity and legal events

Operational support across loan-document review, account reconciliation, delinquency, loss-mitigation administration, foreclosure tracking, bankruptcy and post-foreclosure records.

Axentra works within client servicing rules, investor requirements, systems, templates and decision paths. Axentra does not act as a lender, mortgage servicer, debt collector, foreclosure counsel, title adviser, broker or asset manager.
