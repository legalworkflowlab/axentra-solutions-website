Primary service line

Mortgage Servicing and Default Operations

Operational support across loan-document administration, account reconciliation, delinquency, loss mitigation, foreclosure and bankruptcy workflows.

A mortgage file changes as payments are applied, escrow items are adjusted, borrower circumstances change, documents are received, loss-mitigation options are evaluated, legal action begins or bankruptcy interrupts the ordinary servicing path.

Axentra keeps the loan record, borrower information, payment history, servicing event, legal milestone, exception and next action aligned within the client's operating environment. Delivery follows client-approved servicing rules, investor requirements, systems, templates and escalation routes.

01

Loan and document onboarding

Match the source documents to the system record

The onboarding record identifies missing collateral material, data differences and servicing-transfer exceptions without providing a title opinion.

Loan and document onboarding scope
  • Loan boarding support
  • Borrower and property-data validation
  • Promissory note, mortgage and deed-of-trust information
  • Assignment, allonge and endorsement records
  • Title-policy information and legal description
  • Loan number and investor identification
  • Origination, modification and forbearance documents
  • Payment-history setup
  • Escrow, insurance and tax information
  • Missing-document and collateral exception trackers
  • Repository records
  • Source-to-system validation
  • Servicing-transfer review
  • Data and document reconciliation

Mortgage calculations and account reconciliation

Keep balances, advances and payment history explainable

Calculations follow the client's approved servicing methodology, loan documents, investor requirements, applicable system rules and authorised review process.

Calculation and reconciliation scope
  • Unpaid principal balance
  • Principal and interest breakdown
  • Interest-accrual worksheet and per-diem interest
  • Escrow balance, shortage and surplus
  • Tax and insurance advances
  • Late fees, recoverable fees and expenses
  • Corporate advances
  • Suspense amounts and unapplied funds
  • Delinquency amount
  • Pre-petition and post-petition arrears
  • Reinstatement and payoff amount support
  • Payment-history reconciliation
  • Modification trial payments and modified payment
  • Capitalised amount and deferred balance
  • Forbearance and repayment-plan information
  • Shortfall and deficiency data
  • Claim and recovery worksheets
  • Investor reporting reconciliation

Axentra does not independently determine amounts legally recoverable from a borrower.
