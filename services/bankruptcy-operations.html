Primary service line

Bankruptcy Operations

Connected support across pre-filing preparation, financial calculations, filing readiness and post-filing case administration.

Bankruptcy work does not begin with filing the petition or end when documents are submitted to the court. Information moves from client intake into financial schedules, creditor records, calculations, forms, filing requirements, trustee requests, claims, hearings, amendments, payments and case-closing activities.

Axentra keeps the source information, calculation, form, deadline, required action, review point and case status connected throughout the workflow. Delivery follows the law firm's, lender's, servicer's or authorised client team's systems, instructions, templates and review structure.
