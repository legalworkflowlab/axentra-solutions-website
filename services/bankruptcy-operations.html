Bankruptcy Operations

Pre-filing and post-filing bankruptcy operations

Pre-filing, calculation, filing-readiness and post-filing services for law firms, creditors, lenders and servicers.

Delivery is performed within the scope, systems, templates, instructions and legal-review arrangements agreed with the client.

01

Before filing

Pre-filing operations

The team organises the information and documents needed for attorney review and petition preparation.

Pre-filing activities
  • Matter intake
  • Client questionnaires
  • Document-request lists
  • Document collection and indexing
  • Income and expense information
  • Asset and liability information
  • Creditor details
  • Missing-information follow-up
  • Inconsistency and exception records
  • Data entry into client-approved bankruptcy software
  • Attorney-review package preparation

02

Financial work

Calculations and schedules

Worksheets follow client instructions, current source information and the legal treatment provided by the responsible attorney.

Calculation and schedule activities
  • Six-month income review
  • Current monthly income worksheets
  • Median-income comparison
  • Chapter 7 means-test preparation
  • Chapter 13 disposable-income preparation
  • Schedule I and Schedule J preparation and reconciliation
  • Secured-debt and arrears information
  • Asset, liability and equity worksheets
  • Plan-payment worksheet preparation under client instructions
  • Formula and source checks

Legal conclusions, chapter selection, exemptions, claim treatment and filing approval remain within the client's authorised legal-review process.

03

Preparing the package

Filing readiness

Documents, forms and open items are brought together for the client's legal review and approval.

Filing-readiness activities
  • Petition and schedule preparation
  • Statement preparation
  • Creditor matrix
  • Local forms
  • Filing-fee documents
  • Credit-counselling certificate
  • Signature tracking
  • Redaction checks
  • Cross-form checks
  • Missing-field review
  • Filing-package organisation
  • Attorney approval tracking

04

After filing

Post-filing case operations

Post-filing work covers court information, trustee requests, claims, hearings, payments, case events and closure records. Axentra tracks the action, deadline and status in the client's working environment.

Post-filing activities
  • Filed-case setup
  • Court and case information
  • Docket monitoring
  • PACER monitoring
  • Deficiency tracking
  • Trustee document requests
  • Section 341 meeting tracking
  • Hearing and deadline tracking
  • Claims register monitoring
  • Proof-of-claim records
  • Amendments
  • Chapter 7 event tracking
  • Chapter 13 plan and payment tracking
  • Mortgage cure information
  • Motions and objections tracker
  • Discharge tracking
  • Dismissal, conversion and closure records
  • Final file organisation
  • Case-status reporting

05

Claims and loan records

Creditor, lender and servicer operations

The team matches the bankruptcy case with the borrower and loan record, prepares claim information and tracks payment and case updates. Legal strategy, pleading signature and court representation remain with the client's counsel.

Explore Mortgage Servicing and Default Operations
Creditor, lender and servicer activities
  • Bankruptcy-notice intake
  • Borrower and loan matching
  • Automatic-stay records
  • Proof-of-claim preparation
  • Principal, interest, escrow and arrears reconciliation
  • Payment-history review
  • Plan and confirmation-order abstraction
  • Pre-petition and post-petition payment tracking
  • Payment-change notice preparation
  • Post-petition fee and expense records
  • Final cure and payment records
  • Dismissal, discharge and closure updates
