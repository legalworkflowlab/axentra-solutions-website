Security & Trust

How Axentra protects client information

Information security is addressed through employment and vendor obligations, internal policies, workforce training, approved IT assets, access controls and engagement requirements.

Quality and security responsibilities are considered together during delivery setup. Team members receive access only to information and systems required for assigned roles.

Core safeguards

Responsibilities carried through people, systems and providers

People obligations

Confidentiality, data protection, intellectual property, information security and AI-use requirements.

Vendor flow-down

Relevant confidentiality, access, incident, return, deletion and subcontracting restrictions.

Approved access

Devices, user roles, software, storage and remote-working arrangements set for the engagement.

Training and instructions

Policy acknowledgement, workforce training and engagement-specific data-handling requirements.

Information handling

Access follows the assigned role

The safeguards used depend on the information, client systems, contract terms and work being performed.

  • Confidential and commercially sensitive information
  • Personal and sensitive personal data
  • Protected health information where agreed
  • Privileged and restricted records
  • Minimum necessary access
  • Client and approved tools only
  • Retention, return and deletion requirements
  • Access changes and offboarding

Responsible AI use

Client instructions determine permitted use

Technology use is limited by engagement terms, approved tools and access arrangements.

  • Client instructions and contract restrictions
  • Permitted tools and environments
  • Minimum necessary information
  • Professional review of outputs
  • Vendor obligations and escalation
  • No independent model training with client information

Client information is not entered into unauthorised public AI tools or used for independent model training.

Response and closure

Plan for incidents, interruptions and endings

  • Internal incident reporting and escalation
  • Containment, access changes and record preservation
  • Client notification according to engagement terms
  • Staffing, communication and recovery planning
  • Open-work handover and access removal
  • Information return, deletion and retention actions
  • Asset return and continuing obligations
  • Completion records for offboarding and closure

Transparent certification position

Axentra does not claim an information-security or privacy certification it has not obtained. PHI and HIPAA-related roles, safeguards and any Business Associate Agreement are addressed before processing begins.
