How We Work

Understand the work before establishing delivery

Axentra uses design-thinking methods where they help the teams understand users, dependencies and process friction before changes are proposed.

Client feedback continues after work begins. Instructions, training, quality checks and workflows remain open to agreed adjustment throughout the engagement.

Company-wide process

Five stages from discovery to improvement

The process applies across projects, recurring workflows, dedicated teams and secondments.

01

Understand

Review the work, volumes, systems, working hours, experience, deadlines and expected outputs.

02

Design

Agree responsibilities, team roles, instructions, access, review layers, reporting and escalation.

03

Transition

Complete knowledge transfer, training, access, sample work, calibration and initial quality review.

04

Deliver

Perform and review the work, track open items, manage exceptions and report against requirements.

05

Improve

Review quality findings, feedback and delivery trends, then share relevant improvement options.

Team design

Structured for the engagement

Axentra does not apply one fixed team structure to every client or project.

  • Scope and complexity
  • Expected volumes and variability
  • Required skills and seniority
  • Client systems and access
  • Production and review roles
  • Quality-review requirements
  • Working hours and time-zone overlap
  • Reporting and escalation requirements
  • Project duration
  • Planned holiday and leave coverage

Quality management

Review arrangements follow the work

Not every engagement includes every quality layer. The review structure is agreed according to the work and client requirements.

  • Initial sample validation
  • Calibration exercises
  • First-line checks
  • Supervisory review
  • Independent QA where agreed
  • Risk-based sampling
  • Exception and rework registers
  • Root-cause and corrective-action records
  • Client feedback and refresher training

Continuous client input

Client alignment continues throughout delivery

Delivery is not treated as fixed once the project begins.

Axentra keeps agreed feedback routes open through regular reporting, quality discussions, escalation and change control. Changes to client instructions, systems, products or processes are introduced only after client review and agreement.

The aim is to keep the delivery aligned with the requirement and make useful findings visible without promising a particular cost, accuracy or turnaround outcome.
