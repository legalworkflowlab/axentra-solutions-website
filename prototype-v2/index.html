Technology and judgement

Technology assists. People remain accountable.

Axentra works in client and approved platforms and uses automation or AI where the engagement permits it.

  • Document classification and data extraction
  • Clause and document comparison
  • Workflow routing and status tracking
  • Draft preparation from approved templates
  • Validation and quality sampling
  • Reporting and exception identification

Trained professionals review work against source documents, client instructions and agreed quality criteria. Client information is not entered into unauthorised public AI tools or used for independent model training.
