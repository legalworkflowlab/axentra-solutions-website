Privacy Notice

Website enquiries and information handling

This notice describes information handled through the public Axentra website and general contact routes.

Information provided by you

The contact form prepares an email in your own email application. The website does not transmit or store the fields. If you send the email, Axentra receives the name, organisation, work email, service interest, workflow description, workload and contact preference you choose to include.

Axentra also receives information sent directly to its published email addresses or LinkedIn account.

Purpose

Enquiry information is used to respond, understand the stated requirement, arrange a discussion, maintain relevant correspondence and meet legal or security obligations.

Technical information and analytics

The website code does not use analytics, advertising trackers, account profiles or tracking cookies. It does not set optional cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process standard technical request information under its own terms.

Storage, access and service providers

Information sent by email is stored in the applicable email environment and accessed by personnel involved in responding to the enquiry, subject to organisational and provider controls. Hosting and communication providers process information only for the services they provide.

Retention

Enquiry records are retained only for a relevant business, security, contractual or legal purpose, then deleted or retained under the applicable record requirement.

Your choices and rights

You may ask Axentra to access, correct or delete enquiry information, or raise a privacy question. Applicable rights depend on the information and governing law.

Contact

Send privacy requests to info@axentrabusiness.com. Do not include sensitive case, borrower, health, loan or client records in the first message.
