Process and Quality

Quality control signals in managed workflows

Axentra Insights · 13 July 2026 · 4 min read

Exceptions, rework, unclear handoffs and inconsistent records often show where operating controls need attention.

Quality in managed workflows is not only a final check. It is visible in how work is received, categorised, reviewed, escalated and reported.

Signals worth watching

Repeated missing inputs, unclear ownership, inconsistent naming, re-opened tasks, delayed approvals and unmanaged exceptions can all indicate that the process needs clearer controls.

Controls should be practical

Useful controls include checklists, sampling notes, escalation paths, exception logs, reviewer feedback and delivery reports. They help teams identify where the process needs refinement.
