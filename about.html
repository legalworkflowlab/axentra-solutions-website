About Axentra

Axentra is an alternative legal and business process services company serving clients in India and international markets.

We work as an extended specialist team for law firms, in-house functions, lenders, servicers and legal, contract and property technology companies.

Clients engage Axentra for defined projects, on-demand requirements, managed recurring workflows, dedicated teams and professional secondments.

Our teams combine subject knowledge, documented processes, technology, human review and quality controls to deliver the agreed work and make useful improvement opportunities visible.

Vision

The extended specialist team clients rely on

To become the extended specialist team clients rely on for consistent, secure and accountable legal and business process delivery.

Mission

Flexible teams and managed workflows

To provide flexible teams and managed workflows that combine domain knowledge, responsible technology use, human judgement and measurable quality.

Our working principles

Commitments carried into delivery

Responsibilities, review points and ways of working are established for each engagement.

Work as part of the client team

Understand the client's systems, instructions, working hours and expectations before establishing the delivery structure.

Take responsibility for the agreed work

Be clear about roles, outputs, review points, dependencies and escalation.

Build quality into delivery

Set quality criteria, review work consistently and use findings to strengthen later delivery.

Use technology responsibly

Use platforms, automation and AI where they improve the work, while keeping trained people accountable for the output.

Share what the work reveals

Make recurring issues, rework, bottlenecks and improvement opportunities visible to the client.

Protect client information

Carry confidentiality, privacy, intellectual-property, information-security and AI-use requirements through people, vendors, systems and IT assets.
