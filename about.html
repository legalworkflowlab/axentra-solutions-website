About Axentra

Axentra is an alternative legal and business process services company supporting clients in India and international markets.

We provide additional capacity for defined projects, changing workloads and ongoing operations. Delivery is arranged through on-demand support, dedicated teams, managed workflows and seconded professionals working within client teams.

Our current service areas include bankruptcy, mortgage servicing and default support, contract and commercial operations, defined legal support, and legal-tech and AI quality review.

Vision

A dependable service company

To build a dependable legal and business process services company known for clear communication, secure delivery and consistent quality.

Mission

Flexible support for changing needs

To provide clients with trained teams and flexible delivery models for projects, recurring workflows and changing business needs.

How we work

Clear commitments carried into delivery

Team structure, working hours and review responsibilities are arranged for the engagement.

  • Understand the requirement before proposing the team
  • Be clear about scope, responsibility and escalation
  • Protect client information
  • Maintain quality through review and feedback
  • Use technology responsibly
  • Adapt team structure and coverage to the engagement
