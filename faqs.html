General

10 questions

What services does Axentra provide?

Axentra provides Bankruptcy Operations, Mortgage Servicing and Default Operations, Contract and Commercial Operations, Product and AI Operations, Defined Legal Support and Workflow Assessment.

Who does Axentra work with?

The operating model is designed for law firms, legal departments, lenders, servicers, product teams and commercial functions with document-heavy, calculation-sensitive or deadline-driven workflows.

Does Axentra provide legal advice or representation?

No. Axentra is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice, legal representation or court appearances. Legal decisions remain with the client and its authorised professionals.

How does an engagement begin?

It begins with the workflow trigger, source information, systems, decisions, exceptions, outputs and review requirements. Axentra then documents scope, responsibilities, controls and transition dependencies.

How does Axentra prevent context from being lost?

The delivery record links the source, instruction, calculation or work, owner, deadline, review point, exception, evidence and status.

How are exceptions handled?

Missing information, deviations and unresolved questions are recorded, assigned and routed through the agreed escalation path instead of being left in informal messages.

What happens when client instructions change?

The change is recorded, affected work is identified, instructions and quality checks are updated, and open items are recalibrated with the client.

How is a workflow assessed before transition?

Axentra maps the trigger, inputs, systems, roles, client decisions, delivery activities, exceptions, controls and evidence that confirms completion.

What information remains visible to the client?

The agreed reporting model surfaces status, ownership, deadlines, dependencies, exceptions, review items and completion evidence.

How are quality findings used?

Findings are classified and reviewed for recurring causes. They inform instruction, training, workflow, quality-rule and product-feedback changes agreed with the client.

Bankruptcy

12 questions

What pre-filing bankruptcy work does Axentra support?

The public scope covers intake administration, document collation, information validation, source indexing, approved-system data preparation, missing-item tracking and attorney-review packages.

What bankruptcy calculations does Axentra support?

Scope includes income, Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 worksheet support, asset and liability reconciliation, filing-fee records and calculation quality controls using approved methods and current source data.

Does Axentra decide whether a debtor qualifies for Chapter 7 or Chapter 13?

No. Chapter selection, eligibility and legal conclusions remain within the attorney-led client process.

Does Axentra select exemptions?

No. Exemption data is entered or reconciled only under attorney instruction. Axentra does not select exemptions or advise on asset treatment.

What filing-readiness work does Axentra support?

Support includes form and schedule assembly, cross-form reconciliation, document and signature tracking, redaction checks, filing-package organisation and an attorney-review tracker.

What happens after a case is filed?

Post-filing administration connects docket events, trustee requests, deadlines, claims, amendments, payments, hearings and closure records to the client file and responsible owner.

Does Axentra support trustee document requests?

Trustee-request tracking and document-package administration are within the described scope, subject to client instructions and confirmed access. Axentra does not represent a party at meetings.

Does Axentra monitor PACER and court dockets?

Docket-monitoring support is configured only after the PACER or court-access model, permitted role and escalation procedure are confirmed with the client.

Does Axentra support claims administration?

The described scope includes claim collection, abstraction, matching, classification, comparison, reconciliation and deadline reporting under authorised review.

Does Axentra support creditors and mortgage servicers in bankruptcy?

The service includes operational support for notice intake, loan and case matching, claim records, payment monitoring, critical dates and system reconciliation.

Does Axentra prepare proofs of claim?

Axentra supports proof-of-claim data, reconciliation and document packages within the client process. Signature, filing approval and legal positions remain with authorised professionals.

Does Axentra provide legal representation?

No. Axentra does not represent debtors, creditors, lenders or servicers, appear in court or provide litigation strategy.

Mortgage

10 questions

What mortgage workflows does Axentra support?

The published scope covers loan-document onboarding, account reconciliation, delinquency records, loss-mitigation administration, foreclosure milestone tracking and bankruptcy interface.

What mortgage calculations does Axentra support?

Support includes principal, interest, escrow, advances, suspense, arrears, reinstatement, payoff and payment-history worksheets using client-approved servicing methods.

Does Axentra make lending decisions?

No. Axentra does not make credit, underwriting, lending or investor decisions.

Does Axentra approve or deny loss mitigation?

No. Eligibility, credit, legal and investor decisions remain with the authorised lender, servicer, investor or counsel process.

Does Axentra conduct foreclosure?

No. Axentra supports document readiness, referrals, milestones, records and reporting. It does not conduct foreclosure, sign pleadings or provide foreclosure strategy.

Does Axentra collect borrower payments?

The public scope does not include receiving funds, applying payments or conducting collection calls.

How are mortgage and bankruptcy workflows connected?

A bankruptcy filing changes servicing actions, claims, payments, legal holds and loan-system records. Each workflow keeps its own controls while sharing matched case and loan information.

Does Axentra provide title opinions?

No. Document and title-report administration does not constitute title certification, a title opinion or title advice.

Does Axentra support mortgage proof-of-claim preparation?

The described scope includes claim data, mortgage attachment support, balance and arrears reconciliation, payment history and the supporting-document package under authorised review.

Does Axentra support post-foreclosure workflows?

Post-foreclosure and property-transition activities are excluded from the standard public scope until capability, role and licensing requirements are separately confirmed.

Contracts and commercial data

5 questions

What stages of the contract lifecycle does Axentra support?

Scope covers pre-execution intake and review preparation, execution coordination, and post-execution repository, metadata, obligation, renewal, amendment and reporting work.

What commercial information does Axentra abstract?

Examples include fees, variable charges, revenue share, indexation, rebates, credits, milestones, caps, thresholds, taxes and reconciliation rights.

How are commercial conditions and dependencies captured?

Each value is linked to its trigger, basis, threshold, frequency, effective period, adjustment, dependency, exception, notice, owner, evidence and review status.

Does Axentra manage renewals, obligations and amendments?

Those records form part of post-execution support when included in the agreed repository, ownership and reporting scope.

Does Axentra provide contract negotiation support?

Axentra supports approved-template drafting, clause comparison, redline tracking and approval coordination. Negotiating positions, legal judgement and final approval remain with the client.

Product and AI Operations

8 questions

What UAT services does Axentra provide?

Domain and business UAT covers requirements, user journeys, workflows, roles, permissions, documents, approvals, reports, defects, retesting and release evidence.

Does Axentra provide technical or security testing?

No. The service does not include source-code, penetration, performance, security or automated testing.

How does Axentra validate AI clause extraction?

Validation compares the extracted clause, classification, boundaries, parties, dates, conditions, exceptions, amendments and commercial terms with the source document.

How does Axentra validate AI-generated summaries?

Review checks source accuracy, missing conditions, unsupported conclusions, party attribution, defined terms, amendments, dates, values, dependencies and intended-user suitability.

How are recurring AI errors analysed?

Errors are classified by type, field, clause structure, document hierarchy and scenario so repeated patterns inform logic, prompt, playbook and test recommendations.

Does Axentra modify or retrain AI models?

No model-training or retraining capability is claimed. Recommendations go to the client team responsible for legal, product, data or engineering implementation.

What is regression testing?

Regression testing reruns agreed scenarios after a change to check that corrected behaviour remains stable and previously working behaviour has not been affected.

What product teams does Axentra support?

The service is designed for legal, contract, commercial and property technology teams that need domain-led workflow and output assurance.

Security

10 questions

How does Axentra protect confidential information?

The delivery model links confidentiality requirements to people, vendor, access, approved-system, handling, review, incident and closure controls agreed for the engagement.

What controls apply to employees and vendors?

Relevant obligations address confidentiality, data protection, intellectual property, information security, AI use, access restrictions, incidents, return, deletion and continuing duties.

What IT asset controls are maintained?

The applicable asset and access model is evidenced during diligence. Specific endpoint, MFA, VPN, VDI, encryption and media-control claims are not published until verified.

Does Axentra hold ISO, SOC 2 or HIPAA certification?

The website does not claim ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA or another independent certification.

Does Axentra process protected health information?

Any PHI engagement requires the parties, permitted use, safeguards, access environment, incident duties and Business Associate Agreement requirements to be settled before processing.

How does Axentra use AI?

AI use follows client instructions, contractual restrictions, approved tools, data minimisation, access controls, review and escalation. Client data is not put into unauthorised public AI tools.

May a client prohibit AI use?

Yes. Client instructions and contractual restrictions determine whether AI is permitted for an engagement or activity.

Does Axentra use subcontractors?

Subcontractor use is engagement-specific and must be disclosed, authorised where required and covered by relevant contractual restrictions before access is provided.

What happens when a person leaves?

The agreed offboarding process addresses access suspension or removal, asset return, record handling, continuing obligations and evidence of completion.

What happens when an engagement ends?

Closure addresses access removal, open-work handover, client records, retention, return or deletion, asset actions and completion evidence under the agreement.
