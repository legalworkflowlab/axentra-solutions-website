Security & Trust

Controls must follow the information and the work

Delivery requirements are translated into obligations for people, vendors, access, devices, systems, review and incident response.

Axentra describes and evidences the controls actually in place and does not represent itself as independently certified where certification has not been obtained.

Carry obligations through the delivery chain

Engagement readiness addresses the obligations that apply to personnel and relevant providers before access to client information is approved.

IT assets and access

Confirm the technical control set before processing

The asset model, user responsibility, access approval, permitted software, remote-access method, logging, offboarding, return, deletion, recovery and continuity requirements are recorded for the agreed delivery environment.

Before access

Confirm the approved asset and access model, assigned role, permitted systems and client restrictions.

During delivery

Apply the agreed device, authentication, download, media, software and remote-working rules.

When access changes

Suspend or remove access, preserve required records, return assets and complete agreed deletion or reassignment steps.

Specific endpoint, MFA, VPN, VDI, encryption, backup and technical architecture claims remain excluded from this public page until independently verified.

PHI and HIPAA

Role and safeguards first

Where an engagement involves protected health information, the role of each party, permitted use, safeguards, access environment, incident requirements and Business Associate Agreement obligations are addressed before processing begins.

Axentra does not describe itself as HIPAA certified.

Security diligence

Review information, access and delivery requirements before scope is finalised

Security commitments are documented against the client environment and the information involved. Confidential technical architecture is shared only through an appropriate diligence process.
