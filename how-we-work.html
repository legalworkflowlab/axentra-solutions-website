How We Work

Delivery models configured around the client's systems and priorities

The delivery model is configured around the work, systems, access requirements, review points and level of operating responsibility agreed with the client.

1

Define

Confirm the workflow, activities, responsibilities, systems, volumes, dependencies and intended outputs.

2

Configure

Agree the team structure, instructions, access, controls, review requirements, reporting and escalation routes.

3

Transition

Complete knowledge transfer, training, system readiness, pilot checks and initial delivery validation.

4

Run and Review

Manage the agreed workflow, maintain visibility over delivery and exceptions, and review the model using client feedback and operating information.

Operating Responsibility

Scope, controls and decision routes are agreed before scale.

Axentra manages the activities assigned to its delivery team. Client-authorised professionals retain legal, regulatory, financial and commercial decisions.

Instructions

Approved playbooks, templates, system access, reporting expectations and escalation routes.

Controls

Sampling, exception handling, version control, reviewer feedback and delivery reporting.

Decision routes

Defined points for approvals, risk acceptance, negotiation positions and professional judgement.

Delivery information

Operating data used to adjust staffing, cadence, quality checks and reporting formats.
