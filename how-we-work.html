How we work

Delivery starts with understanding the work

Axentra reviews the work, people, systems, deadlines and expected output before proposing the team and delivery plan.

Where useful for the project, design-thinking methods help uncover user needs, dependencies and friction before solutions are proposed. Client feedback continues after work begins, so instructions, training and delivery can be adjusted throughout the engagement.

Company-wide process

Five steps from discussion to ongoing review

The same core process applies across projects, recurring workflows, dedicated teams and secondments.

  1. 01Understand
  2. 02Set up
  3. 03Transition
  4. 04Deliver
  5. 05Review

01

Understand

Review the work, volumes, systems, deadlines, data and expected output.

02

Set up

Agree the team, roles, instructions, access, quality checks, reporting and escalation routes.

03

Transition

Complete knowledge transfer, training, system access, sample work and initial quality review.

04

Deliver

Perform and review the work, track open items and report status under the agreed service structure.

05

Review

Use quality findings, client feedback and recurring issues to improve instructions, training and delivery.

Engagement-specific teams

The team follows the work

Axentra does not apply one fixed team structure to every engagement.

The team is arranged around the work and the client's delivery needs.

What shapes the team

  • Work type
  • Expected volumes
  • Required legal or business knowledge
  • Turnaround requirements
  • Client working hours
  • Production and review responsibilities
  • Quality-control requirements
  • Reporting and escalation needs
  • Project duration
  • Required systems and access

Roles may include

  • Analysts
  • Paralegals
  • Lawyers
  • Legal or business process specialists
  • Quality reviewers
  • Team leads
  • Delivery managers
  • Subject-matter support
